## The entry

> At 6 AM on Saturday my alarm clock screamed like the house was on fire. The only thing burning was my weekend.

One line, 110 characters, roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.

## How it works

`main.py` holds 10 fresh candidate roasts, scores each against the judge's
brief in its published order (on-subject, laugh, freshness, tightness,
voice — weights 5/4/3/2/1), picks the highest-scoring one, validates it
against the gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, single line), and writes
it to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
